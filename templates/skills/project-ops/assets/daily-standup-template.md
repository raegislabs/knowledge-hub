## Template Usage Guide

### Standup Best Practices

**DO:**
- ✅ Keep it timeboxed (15 minutes max)
- ✅ Focus on work, not people
- ✅ Identify blockers early
- ✅ Be specific about tasks
- ✅ Take detailed conversations offline

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Solve problems in standup (parking lot instead)
- ❌ Report to manager (team sync, not status report)
- ❌ Ramble (1-2 minutes per person max)
- ❌ Skip standup (consistency matters)

### Standup Formats

**Classic Format (3 Questions):**
1. What did I do yesterday?
2. What will I do today?
3. Any blockers?

**Alternative: Walking the Board**
- Go through each In Progress issue
- Owner gives quick update
- Identify blockers

**Alternative: Today + Blockers**
1. What's my focus today?
2. Any blockers?
(Skip yesterday - less important)

### Timeboxing Tips

**5-person team, 15-minute standup:**
- 2 minutes per person
- 3 minutes for blocker discussion
- 2 minutes buffer

**If running over time:**
- Move deep discussions to parking lot
- Skip "yesterday" updates
- Focus only on blockers and today

### Blocker Escalation

**Blocker Duration:**
- < 2 hours: Team resolves
- 2-4 hours: Tech lead involved
- > 4 hours: Product owner/manager escalation
- > 1 day: Scope change consideration
