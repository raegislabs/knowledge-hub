## Template Usage Guide

### Milestone Definition Best Practices

**Good milestone characteristics:**
- **Specific:** Clear deliverables, not vague goals
- **Measurable:** Objective success criteria
- **Achievable:** Realistic given resources
- **Time-bound:** Concrete target date
- **Valuable:** Unlocks next phase or provides stakeholder value

**Example - Good Milestone:**
- **Name:** "User Authentication MVP"
- **Deliverables:** Login/logout, password reset, session management
- **Success Criteria:** 100 test users can authenticate, <2s response time
- **Date:** 2024-03-15

**Example - Bad Milestone:**
- **Name:** "Make progress on auth" (vague)
- **Success Criteria:** "Auth works" (not measurable)

### Milestone Sizing

**Small milestones (1-2 weeks):**
- 3-5 deliverables
- Single team
- Low risk

**Medium milestones (2-4 weeks):**
- 5-10 deliverables
- Cross-functional team
- Moderate risk

**Large milestones (4-8 weeks):**
- 10+ deliverables
- Multiple teams
- High risk
- Consider breaking down

**Too large (>8 weeks):**
- Break into smaller milestones
- Lack of feedback too risky
