## Template Usage Guide

### Report Cadence

**Weekly Reports:**
- Focus on short-term progress
- Tactical details
- Quick blockers
- 1-2 pages max

**Bi-weekly Reports:**
- Balance tactical and strategic
- Sprint summaries
- Medium-term risks
- 2-3 pages

**Monthly Reports:**
- Strategic view
- Milestone progress
- Long-term trends
- 3-5 pages

### Stakeholder Customization

**For Executives:**
- Lead with Executive Summary
- Focus on risks, budget, timeline
- High-level metrics only
- Decision points clear

**For Technical Teams:**
- Detailed progress on stories
- Technical debt and quality metrics
- Architecture decisions
- Dependencies and blockers

**For Product:**
- Feature completion
- User impact
- Scope changes
- Next deliverables

### Status Color Guide

**🟢 Green - On Track:**
- Meeting all targets
- No significant risks
- Schedule maintained
- Quality standards met

**🟡 Yellow - At Risk:**
- Minor delays possible
- Risks identified with mitigation
- May need scope adjustment
- Quality concerns being addressed

**🔴 Red - Off Track:**
- Missing targets
- Critical blockers
- Schedule impact confirmed
- Immediate action required
