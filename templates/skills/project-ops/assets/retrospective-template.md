## Template Usage Guide

### Retrospective Formats

**Format 1: Start/Stop/Continue**
- Simple and actionable
- Good for newer teams
- Duration: 30-45 minutes

**Format 2: What Went Well / Not Well / Try**
- Balanced and comprehensive
- Good for established teams
- Duration: 45-60 minutes

**Format 3: 4Ls (Liked, Learned, Lacked, Longed For)**
- Focus on learning and growth
- Good for experimental sprints
- Duration: 45-60 minutes

**Format 4: Sailboat**
- Visual metaphor (wind=good, anchor=bad, island=goal, rocks=risks)
- Good for team engagement
- Duration: 60 minutes

### Facilitation Best Practices

**DO:**
- ✅ Set positive, blame-free tone
- ✅ Timebox each section
- ✅ Focus on specific, recent examples
- ✅ Create action items with owners
- ✅ Follow up on previous action items
- ✅ Ensure everyone participates

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Let it become a blame session
- ❌ Discuss people, focus on process
- ❌ Let one person dominate
- ❌ End without clear action items
- ❌ Skip the retro when "too busy"

### Action Item Quality

**Good action items are:**
- **Specific:** "Reduce PR review time to < 4 hours" (not "improve reviews")
- **Measurable:** Include success metric
- **Owned:** Single person responsible
- **Timebound:** Due date or sprint number
- **Achievable:** Can be done in 1-2 sprints

**Bad action items:**
- Vague: "Communicate better"
- No owner: Team-wide responsibility
- No metric: Can't tell if it worked
- Too big: Would take months
